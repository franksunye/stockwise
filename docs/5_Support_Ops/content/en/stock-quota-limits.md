Watchlist limits in international v1 are intentionally fixed:

- **Free**: 3 symbols  
- **Go**: 10 symbols  
- **Plus**: 10 symbols

---

### Why limits exist

#### 1) Execution focus
Most users do worse when they monitor too many tickers. The limit keeps the watchlist actionable instead of turning it into noise.

#### 2) Compute fairness
Each watched symbol consumes recurring analysis resources (data refresh + reasoning). Slot limits protect stability and keep response quality consistent for all users.

#### 3) Tier clarity
The 3 / 10 / 10 structure is the current v1 product contract used across pricing, onboarding, and support.

---
