Need to open a specific symbol directly?

Use a deep-link query in the URL and jump straight to the target context, instead of searching manually again. This is also the recommended way to share a symbol brief with teammates.

### Practical benefit
- Faster re-entry from notifications or shared links
- Less context loss between users
- Fewer screenshot-based misunderstandings
