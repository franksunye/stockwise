# Free vs Go vs Plus: What's Different in v1?

For the international app, the current production scope is **v1 tiers only**: **Free**, **Go**, and **Plus**.

---

### Free (Community)
- **Watchlist Capacity**: 3 symbols  
- **Engine Profile**: rule-weighted + lightweight AI summary  
- **Best For**: learning the workflow and building basic review discipline

### Go (Core)
- **Watchlist Capacity**: 10 symbols  
- **Engine Profile**: DeepSeek reasoning path with deeper tactical narrative  
- **Best For**: users who want clearer "why" behind each action suggestion

### Plus (Upcoming in v1 line)
- **Watchlist Capacity**: 10 symbols  
- **Engine Profile**: DeepSeek + Gemini consensus-style reasoning  
- **Best For**: users who want stronger cross-checking before execution

---

### About Pro / Alpha
`Pro` (v2) and `Alpha` (v3) belong to later product stages and are **not part of the current international v1 support scope**.
