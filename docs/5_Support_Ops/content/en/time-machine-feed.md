Time Machine is built to fight hindsight bias.

When you scroll back, you are not looking at "after-the-fact storytelling."  
You are looking at the model output in its original market context.

### Why it matters
- It prevents fake confidence from retrospective chart reading.
- It helps you judge process quality, not just final price outcome.
- It makes validation honest: what was known then vs what is known now.
