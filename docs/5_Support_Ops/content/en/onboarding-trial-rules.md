The onboarding trial is designed to let you feel the v1 workflow before subscribing.

### During trial
- You can experience deeper reasoning than the base Free view.
- You can test Tactical Brief structure with real symbols.
- Trial duration may vary by campaign or invite source.

### When trial ends
- Account returns to your active plan scope (usually Free if no subscription).
- Watchlist and account history stay in place.
- Features outside your current tier are gated again.

### Extension paths
- Subscribe to **Go** inside the app.
- If referral rewards are active, invited growth actions may grant extra trial days.

---

Support note: trial policy can change by campaign window; the in-app user center is the source of truth for your current entitlement.
