> *"Showing you the true lethality of our weapons is the only shortcut to earning your trust."*

### Core Philosophy
ZISO firmly opposes charging monthly software fees before a user has verified our strength. All new users who pass the community screening and complete their initial onboarding configuration will automatically receive a limited-time **ZISO Pro (Full Intelligence & Vision)** trial.

---

### 🔑 Trial Phase: Feature Breakdown

#### 1. Full Compute Unlocked (DeepSeek-V3 Integration)
In the Free version, users can only access the basic `rule-engine` defense. However, during your 3-to-5-day trial period (depending on current campaign rules), your terminal is directly connected to the summit of the ZISO data aggregation network:
-   **AI Cross-Validation**: Consensus-driven insights from multiple models.
-   **Unlimited Query Rate**: No basic-tier throttling.
-   **Detailed Tactical Briefs**: Full reasoning and logical deduction for every signal.

#### 2. Stock Quota Unlock
We temporarily lift the strict watchlist limits of Free accounts. During this trial, we encourage you to add all your historical "baggage" positions and any high-conviction targets to the system for extreme stress testing.

#### 3. Cold Downgrade Execution
Once the trial countdown hits zero, ZISO will not spam you with obtrusive "Pay Now" pop-ups. instead, a silent and cold downgrade occurs:
-   **Overflow Freezing**: Stocks exceeding the Free watchlist limit will be frozen (Read-only, no deep updates).
-   **LLM Detachment**: Access to the large reasoning models is revoked. You fall back to the rigid **L1 Physical Defense** (Traffic lights remain active, but you lose the deep textual reasoning).

### How to Extend the Firepower?
We provide a path to extend your Pro access without payment: visit the **[ Referral Matrix ]** interface within the app. Trade our high-value IP for continued access to our elite compute resources.
