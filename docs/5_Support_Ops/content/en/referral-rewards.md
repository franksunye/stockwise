Referral rewards are campaign-based benefits for inviting qualified new users.

### Typical flow
1. Open **User Center** and copy your invite link  
2. Share with a friend who completes onboarding  
3. If referral rewards are active, extra trial days are granted based on current campaign rules

### Important notes
- Rewards are controlled by operations switches and may change by period.
- Self-referral, duplicate-device abuse, or invalid onboarding paths can be excluded.
- The in-app entitlement panel is the source of truth for final reward results.
