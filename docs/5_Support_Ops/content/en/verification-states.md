ZISO uses three validation states after a signal window evolves:

- **Pending Review**: window is still unfolding
- **Passed**: outcome stays broadly consistent with prior judgment
- **Diverged**: outcome clearly deviates from prior judgment in the review window

This labeling helps users evaluate process quality over time, not just one-off wins.
