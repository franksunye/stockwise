In v1, interaction feedback is immediate by design.

A tap should respond instantly. Animation starts first, then data completes moments later. This keeps execution rhythm stable under real trading pressure.

### What to expect
- Immediate visual response on key actions
- Short async fill for data-heavy surfaces
- Prioritized continuity over blocking waits
