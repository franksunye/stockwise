# 101-08: The 3M Framework (Mind, Method, Money)

> *"Successful trading is based on three pillars: Psychology, Methodology, and Money Management."* — Alexander Elder

The overwhelming majority of beginners enter the market looking for just one M: **Method**. They obsess over "Golden Crosses," "Secret Indicators," or "Insider Tips."

But the reality is cold: if you don't have the support of the first two M's, even the most perfect method will leave you with a zero balance.

---

## 🧘 The First M: Mind (Psychology)
**Weight: 60%**
This is the base of the pyramid. If you fear when losing and get greedy when winning, your own brain becomes your most dangerous opponent.
**The core of Mind is: Accepting probability, not chasing certainty.**

---

## 🛡️ The Second M: Money (Risk Management)
**Weight: 30%**
This determines how long you survive. Many retail investors pick the right stocks but get wiped out by a normal 5% pullback simply because they were "all-in" with margin.
**The core of Money is: Survival.**

---

## 🛠️ The Third M: Method (Strategy)
**Weight: 10%**
This is the tip of the pyramid—and the least important part. If your risk control is solid and your mind is as steady as a mountain, you can make money in the long run even by flipping a coin, provided you have a positive risk/reward ratio.

---
