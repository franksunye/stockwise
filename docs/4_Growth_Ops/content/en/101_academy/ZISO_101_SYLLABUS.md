# ZISO 101 Academy Syllabus: The Path to Rational Execution

> "The market is not a place for predictions; it is a place for discipline."

This syllabus defines the vertical evolution of a trader, from emotional gambling (L0) to systematic mastery (L4). Each article is designed to dismantle a specific cognitive bias or provide a concrete tool for defense.
