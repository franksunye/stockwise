# Epilogue — Finding Your Quant Oasis

> "Real heroism is to see the world as it is, and to love it." — Romain Rolland

### 1. The Truth: The "Insurmountable Wall" for Retail Investors

Having read this series, you may feel a slight chill.

The physical hegemony of L4, the computing fortress of L3, the cognitive traps of L0 and L1... as an ordinary investor, it seems there is no chance of winning in this Asura field.

That sense of crisis is correct. **This is also the original intention behind the birth of ZISO AI—to tell you where the mines are and where the oases are.**

### 2. Breakthrough: Why Level L2 is the Best "Safe Haven" for You?

Despite the extreme power of L3 and L4, they share a common weakness: **They cannot accommodate small-scale flexibility.**

Top-tier funds, due to their massive size, cannot flexibly enter and exit high-efficiency local rules. This is the excellent territory where ZISO AI establishes its defensive line at Level L2.

We can learn risk attribution from L3 funds and high-precision execution logic from L4, and then simplify them into a **"Logic Closed Loop"** that ordinary people can truly execute.

### 3. Action: Your Next Steps

Climbing the pyramid is not an overnight task, but you now at least have a direction:
-   **Step 1**: Completely bury intuition (L0). Stop saying "I think."
-   **Step 2**: Upgrade your toolbox (L1). See through the lagging shadows of indicators.
-   **Step 3**: Embrace rules (L2). Let ZISO AI become the protective net for your logic.

### 4. Summary: Welcome to the Real World

The Quant Maturity Pyramid is not meant to make you feel despair, but to help you **choose your battlefield wisely**.

May every one of your future trades be clear about which layer you are in, why you win, and why you stop a loss. There are no miracles here—only continuously evolving logic.
