## 五、封面图执行稿

推荐方向：

- 不出现人物，不出现脸
- 中心是一部手机或一张社交信息流卡片
- 中央只有一张被放大、被高亮的赚钱截图
- 周围是大量发灰、下沉、几乎被忽略的失败样本
- 第一眼先让人感觉“满屏都在晒赚钱”
- 第二眼再意识到“其实只有赢家被看见了”

The meaning must be obvious in one second: you are only seeing the winner who is still visible, not the full crowd who already lost. Use a premium editorial finance-magazine still-life style with lighter premium palette, soft neutral background, clean lighting, strong foreground-background separation, and no poster drama.
